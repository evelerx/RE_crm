Privacy Policy

Northstone CRM helps real-estate professionals manage leads, deals, contacts, tasks, reports, integrations, and builder websites. This policy explains the basic information we collect and how we use it.

Information We Collect

  • Account details such as name, email, phone number, company, city, role, and subscription plan.
  • CRM data you add, including contacts, deals, tasks, notes, property details, uploaded images, and reports.
  • Operational data such as login time, feature usage, support messages, audit logs, and security events.
  • Integration data only when you connect a provider, such as Google Workspace permissions for Gmail, Calendar, or Meet.

How We Use Information

We use your information to provide the CRM, keep accounts secure, support subscriptions, power reporting, improve product reliability, and respond to support or demo requests.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We may use trusted service providers for hosting, database storage, email delivery, analytics, payment processing, and integrations required to operate Northstone CRM.

Security

We use access controls, encrypted connections, audit logging, and admin-managed permissions to protect account data. Users should keep passwords private and remove access for team members who no longer need the CRM.

Data Retention

We keep account and CRM data while your account is active or as needed for legal, billing, security, and operational reasons. Demo accounts may be automatically deleted after the demo period ends.

Your Choices

You can request account updates, deletion, or privacy support by contacting us. Some business records may need to be retained for legal, security, or billing purposes.
