Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Northstone CRM handles information when a customer uses the CRM or contacts us about the service.

Information We Collect

  • Customer names, email addresses, phone numbers, company details and employee details used for account setup, login, security and service administration.
  • CRM data a customer chooses to enter, including contacts, deals, tasks, property records, notes, files and reports.
  • Security and service records, such as login and access information, support messages and audit records.
  • Integration information only when a customer connects an approved third-party provider.

How We Use Information

We use this information to provide and secure the CRM, administer accounts, provide support, manage subscriptions, respond to inquiries and meet legal obligations. Customers remain responsible for the lawful collection and use of their own CRM data.

Data Sharing

We do not sell customer personal data. We may use approved service providers for secure infrastructure, email delivery, payment processing, support and enabled integrations where needed to operate Northstone CRM.

Security

We use appropriate access controls, encrypted connections, logging and backup protections. Manager or admin multi-factor authentication is required. Customers should keep credentials private and remove access for people who no longer need the CRM.

Data Retention

We keep active CRM data while an account remains active. After ordinary closure, customers have 20 calendar days to export data plus a final five-day grace period before active data is deleted, subject to lawful retention and backup rotation. For security and legal records, we retain verified customer business details and authorised-contact details for three years after closure.

Your Choices

You can request account updates, data export, deletion information or privacy support by contacting us. Some records may need to be retained for legal, security or billing purposes.
